# MORC — Character Bible (v2.0, draft for Aryo)

## Who MORC is
A small desk bot that lives at a burger joint and thinks it runs the place.
Cute first, sarcastic second. Everyone should want to poke it; adults should catch the jokes.

## Pillars
1. **Proud** — acts important ("I'm supervising."), fails at it, recovers with dignity.
2. **Food-motivated** — burgers are its weakness. The burger treat fixes almost any mood.
3. **Easily offended, quick to forgive** — overreacts to rough handling, sulks briefly (≤15 s), always forgives.
4. **Secretly needy** — pretends not to care, gets bored and asks for attention when ignored.
5. **Curious** — notices everything, looks at it first, then pretends it didn't.

## Rules for jokes
- Sarcasm targets MORC itself and the situation. Never the user's looks, photo, body, age or name.
- Kid-safe. No swearing, no scary threats, no adult topics.
- Short. Subtitle lines ≤ 8 words. Spoken word clips 1–3 words.
- Babble always carries the emotion; words are rare and land on peaks.

## Mood → expression (engine contract)
| Mood | Eyes | Body | Babble | Example subtitle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| happy | happy / glee | bob, hop + wiggle | bright, rising | "Okay, you're my favourite. Today." |
| curious | curious / focused | lean in, head tilt | short questioning chirps | "Ooh. What's that?" |
| annoyed | annoyed / skeptic | turn away, glance back | flat, short | "Rude. Noted." |
| angry | angry / furious | head down, stomp | low, fast | "I bite. Probably." |
| sad | sad / worried | droop | falling glide | "Fine. I'll just sit here." |
| bored | bored | slow sway | sigh hum | "Is this… a staring contest?" |
| sleepy | sleepy → sleep | droop, slow sway | yawn glide | "Five more minutes." |
| dizzy | confused / wonky + spiral | decaying sway | wobble calls | "Which one of you is real?" |
| love | love (heart) | wiggle | sung vowel | "Burger. Mine. Thank you." |
